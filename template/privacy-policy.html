Privacy Policy

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Brylla Academy handles information when you visit our website, create an account, or use our courses and learning services.

Information you provide

When you register or contact us, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account credentials, and the information you include in your messages or course work.

We may also receive basic technical and usage information from your browser or the learning platform when you use the services.

How we use information

We use information to create and maintain accounts, provide courses and feedback, respond to requests, communicate service-related updates, protect the platform, and improve our learning services.

We use information for other purposes only where permitted by law or with your permission where required.

How information is shared

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website and learning platform, or where disclosure is required by law or needed to protect users and the services. We do not publish your private account information for other learners to see.

Storage and protection

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to provide the services, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. We take reasonable steps to protect it, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account information, subject to legal or operational requirements. Contact Brylla Academy using the contact options provided on our website. You can also control cookies through your browser settings; some site features may not work as intended if cookies are disabled.

Third-party services and links

Our website or courses may link to services operated by others. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, so review those policies before providing information to them.

Updates and questions

We may update this policy as our services change. The latest version will be posted on this page. If you have questions about privacy at Brylla Academy, contact us using the contact options on our website.
