Bookkeeping

A six-week US bookkeeping program. Record transactions, work in QuickBooks Online and Xero, reconcile accounts, and close a client's books.

  • Tuition GHS 950
  • 6 weeks
  • 18 recorded lessons
  • Next cohort: date to be announced
Enroll now All courses

Six modules, one each week. Every module has three recorded lessons of 45 minutes, one live session of up to an hour, an assignment, and a quiz.

Week 1: Bookkeeping foundations

  • What a US bookkeeper does
  • Debits, credits, and journal entries
  • Chart of accounts
  • Live session: Chart of accounts build-off
  • Assignment: Journal entries, a trial balance, and a chart of accounts cleanup

Week 2: QuickBooks Online

  • QuickBooks Online setup and navigation
  • Bank feeds, bank rules, and categorizing
  • Accounts receivable
  • Live session: The shoebox challenge
  • Assignment: Set up the client and complete February bookkeeping in QuickBooks Online

Week 3: Payables and reconciliations

  • Accounts payable
  • Bank reconciliation
  • Credit card reconciliation
  • Live session: Find the difference
  • Assignment: Accounts payable, bank, and credit card reconciliations

Week 4: Assets, loans, and adjustments

  • Fixed assets and depreciation
  • Amortization, prepaid expenses, and accruals
  • Loan accounts
  • Live session: Schedule lab
  • Assignment: Depreciation, amortization, and loan schedules
  • Quiz and mid-course test covering weeks 1 to 4

Week 5: Xero and merchant apps

  • Xero from start to finish
  • Stripe
  • PayPal and reconciling merchant payouts
  • Live session: Messy e-commerce month
  • Assignment: Xero bookkeeping, plus Stripe and PayPal reconciliation

Week 6: Month-end close and career

  • Month-end close and financial reports
  • Real-world practice: cleanup and catch-up
  • Job sites, applications, and using AI
  • Live session: Capstone presentations and mock interviews
  • Capstone: Close the client's first-quarter books and present the results
  • Final test covering all modules

Each assignment builds the same client's first-quarter books. In week 6, students correct earlier work, close March, prepare the financial statements, write a summary to the owner, and present the results. A certificate is awarded with an overall score of 70% or more and a submitted capstone.
