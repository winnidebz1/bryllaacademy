How to use the learning platform

This short guide shows where to find lessons, assignments, and your progress. Each module appears when the founder opens it.

  1. Open a lesson. Use the list beside the player. Start here stays open after you sign in. Week 1 through Week 6 stay hidden until the founder opens that module.
  2. Watch the lesson. The video plays in the panel above this guide. If a lesson does not have a video yet, the page says so.
  3. Mark it complete. When you finish the current lesson, choose Mark complete. The progress bar counts the lessons you have finished.
  4. Submit an assignment. Open the week in the lesson list and choose Submit assignment. Add the files your instructor asked for. Each week accepts one submission.
  5. Track your progress. The percentage at the top of the lesson list shows how many lessons are complete. Your student dashboard shows the same progress after you leave this page.
